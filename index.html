Medo Call Recorder

Privacy Policy

This policy explains how Medo Call Recorder handles information, call recordings, permissions, and subscription verification.

Last updated: October 20, 2026

1. Information We Handle

Medo Call Recorder is designed to record calls and manage recordings on your device. The information handled by the app depends on the features you use and the permissions you grant.

  • Call recordings created using the app.
  • Contacts information, if you grant access, to support contact-related features such as filtering or ignoring selected contacts.
  • Subscription keys and verification responses when you use subscription-protected features.
  • Local application logs related to subscription status and abuse prevention.

2. Call Recordings and Local Storage

Call recordings are stored locally on your device in the storage location selected or configured in the app. The app is designed not to upload your recordings to the developer's servers.

You are responsible for managing your recordings, choosing a secure storage location, and deleting files you no longer need. If you uninstall the app or clear its data, recordings and settings may be affected depending on where they are stored.

3. Permissions and System Access

The app may request permissions or system access needed for its features, including:

  • Contacts: to support contact-based filtering and related functions, if enabled.
  • Notifications: to display app status or relevant notifications, where applicable.
  • Battery optimization settings: to help reduce interruptions to background operation on supported devices.
  • Storage or folder selection: to let you choose where recordings are saved.
  • Call-related system access: to support call-state and audio functionality, depending on your Android version and device configuration.
  • Wireless debugging and Shizuku access: to activate the integrated system-access functionality when required.

Available permissions and behavior may vary by Android version, device manufacturer, and app version. You can review and revoke applicable permissions in your device settings, although doing so may disable related features.

4. Integrated Shizuku Engine

Medo Call Recorder includes an integrated Shizuku engine. Initial activation may require you to complete Android Wireless Debugging pairing or other on-device setup steps shown by the app.

Shizuku provides elevated access to certain Android system APIs within the limits of the granted service and Android's security model. Any permission or access request should be reviewed before you approve it.

Compatible third-party applications may be able to request access through the Shizuku service while it is running. Such applications are separate from Medo Call Recorder, and their data practices are governed by their own privacy policies.

5. Subscription Verification and Firebase

Some features may require a subscription key. When you enter or validate a key, the app connects to Firebase-backed services to check the key's validity, status, or previous use.

  • The subscription key is sent for verification.
  • The app's verification service may return information about the key's status.
  • The app is designed, according to its current implementation, to send the subscription key rather than an Android ID or device identifier for this check.
  • Network access is required for online verification. Other recording features may work locally, subject to the app's requirements.

Requests to Firebase are processed by the relevant service infrastructure. Please review Google's privacy information for details about its services. Do not enter a subscription key that you are not authorized to use.

Google Privacy Policy: policies.google.com/privacy

6. Local Logs and Abuse Prevention

The app may maintain local log or state files containing subscription status and information needed to help prevent misuse. These files are stored on your device as part of the app's local operation.

Local logs may be removed when you clear app data or uninstall the app, depending on their storage location. Do not modify these files if doing so could disrupt app functionality.

7. Data Sharing

The app is designed not to sell your personal information or send your call recordings to the developer. Subscription verification involves communication with Firebase-backed services as described above.

Information may be processed by service providers involved in delivering the features you request, in accordance with their applicable terms and privacy policies. This policy does not cover independent applications or services that you choose to use.

8. Data Retention and Deletion

Call recordings remain on your device until you delete them or they are removed by your own device or storage-management actions. You can delete recordings using the app or your device's file manager, depending on the storage location.

Local logs and app settings may remain until you clear the app's data or uninstall it. Subscription verification records handled by Firebase-backed services may be retained according to the service configuration and applicable policies.

9. Security

Keeping recordings on your device helps you retain control over them, but no storage method is completely risk-free. Protect your device with a secure screen lock, keep Android and the app updated, and carefully review permissions granted to this and other applications.

The developer cannot guarantee the security of your device, external storage, third-party apps, or services outside the developer's control.

10. Children's Privacy

Medo Call Recorder is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided information through a service used by the app, take appropriate steps with the relevant service provider.

11. Your Responsibilities and Applicable Laws

Call recording laws vary by country and region. You are responsible for obtaining any consent required by law and using the app in compliance with applicable privacy, telecommunications, and recording laws.

Do not record or share calls unlawfully or use the app to violate another person's rights.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This policy may be updated when app features, data handling, or legal requirements change. Updates will be published on this page, and the “Last updated” date will be revised when appropriate. You should review this page periodically.

13. Contact and Questions

This privacy policy is published with the Medo Call Recorder project. For questions about the app, refer to the project's official distribution page or repository.
